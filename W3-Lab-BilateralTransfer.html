Bilateral Transfer Tracing Task

In this task you will trace a winding channel on screen as quickly as you can while staying inside the walls. You will do it with both hands — before and after a block of practice with your dominant hand only.

Your score for each trace is the time it takes to get from START to END — a faster time means better performance. To keep it fair, accuracy is enforced: if your line leaves the channel at any point, the trace does not count and you redo it. So only clean, in-channel traces are recorded, and a fast time can never come from rushing sloppily. Follow the steps in order; the app tells you which hand to use each time.

How to trace

Go as fast as you can while staying inside the walls. Touching or crossing a wall voids the trace — you’ll simply redo it until you get a clean run. Early traces with your non-dominant hand may take a few tries; that’s expected.

Before you start

Use a mouse, trackpad, or touchscreen — whatever you would normally use. Use the same input device for the whole session. Find a quiet spot; you will do about 16 short traces. It takes 10–15 minutes.
